Seasonal Maintenance Guide

Keep your engine-driven welder running at peak performance with our seasonal checklists. Preventative care today saves expensive repairs tomorrow.

🌷 Spring Wake-Up

Routine

Get your machine ready for the busy season.

  • Change engine oil and filter
  • Inspect air filter for winter nesting/dirt
  • Test battery voltage and charge
  • Check fuel stability and clear lines
  • Inspect all belts and hoses for cracks
  • Clean cooling fins and airflow paths

☀️ Summer Heat

Routine

Prevent overheating during peak summer loads.

  • Weekly air filter checks (dusty sites)
  • Monitor oil levels more frequently
  • Inspect fuel lines for heat-related degradation
  • Clean generator housing and intake screens
  • Check for coolant leaks (on liquid-cooled units)

🍂 Fall Wind-Down

Routine

Protect your machine before the first freeze.

  • Post-season oil and filter change
  • Add fuel stabilizer to the tank
  • Replace spark plugs for next season
  • Inspect fuel tank for leaks or corrosion
  • Grease all applicable chassis points

❄️ Winter Storage

Routine

Ensure a smooth start come springtime.

  • Store in a dry, sheltered, covered area
  • Disconnect battery or use a trickle charger
  • Apply protective coating to exposed metal parts
  • Log all maintenance performed this year
  • Store in a temperature-controlled area if possible
